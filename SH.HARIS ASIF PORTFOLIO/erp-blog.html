FACTORY ERP SYSTEM

The Factory ERP System is a centralized business management solution designed to simplify and organize factory operations. It brings essential processes such as production, inventory, sales, purchasing, employee management, and reporting into one unified platform.

The system provides a clean and intuitive dashboard where users can monitor important business activities, track inventory, manage production, and access real-time operational information. This reduces dependency on scattered records and makes day-to-day management more structured and efficient.

This project demonstrates how modern software can transform traditional factory workflows into a connected digital system. The focus was on creating a professional, scalable, and user-friendly ERP experience that helps businesses manage their operations with greater clarity and control.
